<h2>🧠 About Me</h2>

<em>

I am a **visionary and builder** driven by curiosity, technology, and the desire to turn ideas into real-world products.

I enjoy thinking beyond individual technologies. My focus is on understanding **problems, systems, opportunities, and how different technologies can be combined to create something meaningful.**

I explore a wide range of fields including **software engineering, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, computer science, systems, networking, hardware, IoT, game development, graphics, 3D, animation, design, and multimedia.**

I don't want to be limited to a single technical discipline. I want to understand enough across different domains to **design better solutions, recognize opportunities, build prototypes, and lead ideas from concept to reality.**

Technology is the medium.
The real goal is **innovation, problem solving, and building things that matter.**

I may not personally write every line of code in every project. What matters is understanding the system deeply enough to **define the vision, make the critical decisions, build what needs to be built, and bring the right people and technologies together.**

**Think. Explore. Design. Build. Iterate.**

</em>
