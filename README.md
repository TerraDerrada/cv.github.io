<legend align="left"><h3>🧠 About Me</h3></legend>

<em>

I am a **lifelong learner** with an endless curiosity for understanding how things work.

My biggest hobby is **studying**. I enjoy continuously learning new subjects, exploring unfamiliar technologies, understanding fundamentals, experimenting with ideas, and turning knowledge into practical projects.

**Learn. Understand. Experiment. Build. Repeat.**
